# 5. Conclusion

The fitting corpus shifts the language of the readout, and the model keeps a preference of its own. Gemma readouts stay English even through a Chinese-fitted lens, Qwen readouts let Chinese through an English-fitted one, and the preference is family-stable from 0.6B to 32B. The two lenses are not interchangeable: layers that go dark under one lens carry words under the other. What the two lenses share is the same concept at the same layers, and an agreement that goes far beyond identity. This is consistent with near-semantic content in the readout layers, with the caveat that whole tokens are not informationally equivalent across languages. Any claim that reads a model’s internal states through a fitted lens should state what corpus the lens was fitted on. Any claim about the language of a model’s thinking should first establish that the language observed is not the language of the lens, and then establish which language remains when the lens is turned away.
