# Appendix figures

Figures A1–A63 of the manuscript *Thinks Like a Bilingual: Language Models Have Their Preferred Languages in the Global Workspace* (Liu, Nelyubina, Myachykov, Cambria & Zhang), reproduced from the submission file. Figure numbering follows the manuscript appendix. Each image is the 600-dpi render used in the manuscript, downsampled to 1800 px wide for the web.

## How to read the figures

Figures A1–A60 are per-layer trajectories of the candidate pool, one figure per (model family, probe) pair. Within each figure the rows are the models of one family and the columns are four panels. The first two give the language composition of the English-fit and Chinese-fit candidate pools as stacked bars that sum to one at every layer; the bands are English, Chinese, other natural languages, and non-language tokens. The third panel is cross-language overlap without order (solid: identical or translated partners; dashed: identical partners only). The fourth is overlap under order, again as a nested pair (cross-language solid, within-language dashed). Figures A1–A20 use the top-100 candidates, A21–A40 the top-5, and A41–A60 the top-10. Figures A61–A63 repeat the four overlap measures of Figure 2 of the paper for the france-capital, sport and spider-legs probes, on the top-100 pools at the last position.
